Your data stays yours

Privacy Notice

Compass stores all of your data locally on your device using your browser's local storage. Your health information — including cycle data, symptoms, journal entries, lab results, and check-ins — never leaves your device.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal or health information. There are no accounts, no servers storing your data, and no third-party data sharing.

Clearing your browser cache or app data will permanently delete your Compass data. We recommend using the Export feature in the Reflect tab to back up your information periodically.

By continuing, you acknowledge this zero-knowledge, local-first privacy design.
